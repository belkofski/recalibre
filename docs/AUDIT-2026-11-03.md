# Recalibre — Verified website audit

Date: 3 November 2026. Subject: https://recalibre.cloud as served that day (Netlify deploy of commit `782c585`, 3 October 2026), plus the `main` branch at `83012d0` where it differs.

Method: every route in the sitemap fetched and parsed; every internal and external link checked; structured data extracted; Google and Bing queried for `site:recalibre.cloud`; home, contact, about and one case page reviewed in a browser at 1440 and 375 px wide; asset weights measured over the wire; PageSpeed Insights run against the live pages; source read where the live site could not show the reason.

This document replaces the working diagnosis of the same date ("RECALIBRE — Website Problems"). Each of its six areas is answered below with what was verified, what was not a problem, and what is a business decision rather than a defect.

Legend: **CONFIRMED** verified on the live site · **PARTLY** true in places · **NOT A DEFECT** checked and fine · **OWNER** needs a decision or material only the owner has · **UNVERIFIED** could not be measured from outside.

## 1. Visual experience

| Claim from the working diagnosis | Verdict | What was seen |
|---|---|---|
| Not enough visual content | PARTLY | **Home** is dense: hero photograph, partner hub, four project cards with real screens, five capability cards each with a visual, OPS spotlight with device plate, three-stage model, insights, FAQ, form. 13 images plus 55 inline SVGs. **Interior pages are thin.** About has no photograph at all. The ABP case page has one image (the client's home page shown whole) and three bullet points. Insights articles are text only. |
| Feels insufficiently alive | OWNER | The site has three kinds of motion: the curtain, masked line reveals on entry, and two carousels. Everything else is still by a recorded design decision ("no cursor follower, nothing loops"). Whether that restraint is right is a brand call, not a defect. |
| Needs stronger visual storytelling | PARTLY | Home tells a story (problem → capabilities → proof → method → FAQ → form). Case pages do not: ABP shows a deliverable and stops. OPS and Contraxis show schematics with "demonstration data" and "not a screenshot" labels, which is honest and also reads as "not real yet". |
| Needs distinctive service visuals | CONFIRMED | Capability cards reuse project plates (a Contraxis schematic, an OPS report, an ABP photograph, a Belkofski cube). No capability has a visual of its own. |
| Better presentation of projects | PARTLY | The project grid on Home and `/work` is good. The case pages underneath are short and uneven: ABP 1 image, OPS and Contraxis long with diagrams, Belkofski medium. |
| Purposeful motion | OWNER | See "alive" above. The one motion that is not purposeful is the curtain: it delays content on every arrival and shows a scrambled wordmark while it counts. |

Also seen: the hero's blue wall photograph is a rendered room, not a Recalibre workplace. It is good, and it is the only atmosphere image on the site.

## What this means for the plan

The working diagnosis was right about the gaps and wrong about the cause. The site is not weak because it is quiet. It is weak because:

1. **Search engines cannot find it.** One indexed page makes every other improvement invisible. Fix first: Search Console, sitemap submission, hreflang, lastmod, and finding out why thirteen pages were skipped.
2. **It has no pages for the things people search for.** Services, industries, and a French version. Those are content and routing work, not animation work.
3. **It may not be able to receive a lead.** Confirm the form sends, today.
4. **It makes the reader wait before it says anything.** The curtain costs more than any animation would gain.
5. **It cannot prove much, and three logos are shown without permission on file.** Either get the permissions and the founder's name on the page, or take the logos down. That is the owner's call and it is the biggest trust item on the site.

Everything in sections 1 to 6 that is marked NOT A DEFECT should be protected during the redesign, not rebuilt.
